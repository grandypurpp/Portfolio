CUSTOM CODE · GOHIGHLEVEL FORMS

Sequential respondent IDs the platform did not offer

A client wanted every form submission numbered in order. GoHighLevel has no setting for it, so I wrote a short script that fills a hidden form field. The number then shows up in the admin view of each submission under Respondent ID.

HOW THE SCRIPT WORKS
STEP BY STEP
  • Reads the last number from the visitor's browser, and starts at 100000 if there is none yet.
  • Fills the hidden Respondent ID field with that number, marks it required, and fires an input event so the form registers the value.
  • Saves the next number (one higher) in the browser for the following form.
  • Shows up in the admin view of each submission under Respondent ID.
Trade-off I would call out: the counter lives in the browser, so numbering is sequential per device. A single account-wide count would need a server-side counter, such as a workflow or webhook.

CRM · MARKETING AUTOMATION

GoHighLevel email event marketing automation

Built email marketing newsletters, all driven by event-based automations specific to the client's nurture sequence.

  • Contact scoring: Scores each contact in multiple areas, showing their buying probability.
    • Survey questions are split into two frameworks, questions 1 to 6 and questions 7 to 12, each with its own if/else conditions.
    • A yes answer earns points: 1 point per question in the first framework and 10 per question in the second, the two sections score very differently. Either answer moves the contact on to the next question.
    • Score thresholds trigger the emails: the first goes to contacts scoring under 9, the second to contacts scoring over 20
    • Workflow sending is throttled.
  • Nectar in the Nurture: Puts the contact into varying automations based on what they click and don't click.
  • Agent Assign: Uses UTM parameters from our campaigns to assign the contact to the right real estate agent, ending the confusion over whose client it is.
